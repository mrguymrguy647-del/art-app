# Sketchbook: the user's own drawings

Photos of drawings by the person this app is built for. They are here because they have what
early agent paintings lacked: line weight, shading that follows the form, a pose with
attitude, energy marks and a joke. `index.json` says, for each, what technique to learn.

**Rules.** These are the user's own drawings, not public domain or CC0, so they are not in the
masterworks index. Most are fan art: the characters belong to their owners. Study how each is
drawn; never redraw the character, and never ship these pictures in an app, game or video.

## What they teach

1. **Two line weights.** A heavy contour in front, light marks behind (`pen-hero-head`).
2. **Shade along the form.** Hair spikes, cloth folds and muscles are hatched in the direction
   they grow or pull, as one block on the shadow side (`prince-profile`, `fighter-crouch`).
3. **A pose is a choice.** Chin up, a fist pushed at the viewer, a raised finger: pick the
   moment, then tilt everything into it (`prince-profile`, `fighter-crouch`, `finger-orb`).
4. **Darkest dark beside whitest white** at the focal point (`face-door`).
5. **Energy marks** radiate from the source and thin out (`fighter-crouch`, `finger-orb`).
6. **A silhouette you could recognise in black** (`golden-hair-mashup`).
7. **An idea with a twist** beats a nice subject: a door with a face, a grumpy face under hero
   hair (`face-door`, `golden-hair-mashup`).

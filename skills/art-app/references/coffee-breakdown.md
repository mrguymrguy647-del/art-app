# Coffee cup

**Shapes.** Three nested ellipses (saucer, cup rim, the coffee's surface), seen from above at an
angle; the dark table fills the corners; the spoon is a small bright shape with a hard
reflection.

**Values** (`coffee-values.png`: black 23%, dark 30%, mid 29%, light 12%, white 5%). Four
groups when squinted. The white rim of the cup is the lightest ring in the picture and sits on
the darkest coffee: the strongest edge is at the subject.

**Palette** (`coffee-palette.png`): one warm key, from near-black through red-brown and orange
to cream. Every colour is on the same side of the wheel; the picture is unified by hue and
separated by value.

**Why it works.** One key, five values, the lights saved for the rim and the spoon. The wood's
grain is large and directional (marks), the porcelain is smooth (flat on purpose): texture
changes where the material changes, which is what tells the eye what things are.

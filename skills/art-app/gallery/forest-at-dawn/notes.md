# Forest at dawn

A new subject from the benchmark (`exercises/benchmark.md`): a path into a misty forest at
dawn, low light from one side, one animal on the path. 1920 x 1080, painted with the phase 4
process in stage order (`process.png`: values, colour, light, detail, finish). The intent: focal
`deer`, light at the end of the path (`[1180, 560]`), mood "still, cold, hopeful", key colours
the dawn, a mist blue and the trunks.

## The stages, and what the senses said at each

- **Values**: a grey sky with a light pool at the end of the path, three rows of trunks in
  three greys, a mid-grey ground fading to the mist, a light path wedge, the deer the darkest
  thing against the lightest. The thumb reads: dark verticals framing a light centre with a
  dark figure.
- **Colour**: a cool key (sky, trunks, ground) with one warm colour (the dawn) at the light;
  `out of key: nothing`. The first version's ground was a flat green plane with a hard horizon
  at the mist and the glow was under the mist; the layers were reordered: ground and path under
  the mid mist, the glow above it.
- **Light and form**: mist as three gradients (far, mid, a blurred band) and a warm radial at
  the light; the glow on screen above the mist; a clipped light on the path; warm edges on the
  trunks nearest the light; five soft rays. The deer's rim of light: a drawn `rim` came out as
  dashes on the jittered polygons and lit the whole head; it is a clipped screen gradient on
  the deer's right edge instead.
- **Detail**: fur strokes on the deer, stones and tufts at the end of the path, bark strokes on
  the near trunks (fewer than first written), ferns in the corners, ragged leaf edges on the
  canopy. Detail went from 10% to 14% of the marks within four cells of the deer; the numbers
  want 3x the area's 8% for a 5 and a small subject in a big landscape does not get there
  without emptying the rest.
- **Finish**: mood pass at 0.16, vignette centred on the light, paper. Final critique: 5 value
  groups, focal contrast 1.2x, the eye goes to the deer, 1 of 1 parts read; three "no light and
  dark side" findings on the far trunks, the mid mist and the canopy, which are flat on purpose
  (mist and silhouettes). Numbers: readability 5, values 5, colour 4, focal point 5, detail 3.
- **Blind read**: reads (`blind-read.md`): "a misty forest ... a small dark silhouette of a
  four-legged animal with upright ears, something like a deer or a young fawn ... my eye goes
  first to the small dark animal ... quiet, hushed".

## What each tool did

- **Trunks:** tapered `path`s past the top edge in three rows and three values; bark as
  `scatter` strokes at 84 to 96 degrees masked to the near trunks' layer; warm side-light as
  gradients masked to the trunk layers.
- **Mist:** vertical gradients with a transparent end on two layers, a blurred band, a warm
  radial near the light; the ground's far colour is the mist's so it has no edge.
- **Glow and rays:** a radial on a screen layer above the mist; five wedge polygons from the
  light in a blurred group on a screen layer at 0.14.
- **Path:** a jittered polygon with a gradient, strokes and stones masked to it, a clipped
  radial light, a faint puddle, the deer's ground shadow.
- **Deer:** eleven shapes (body polygon, neck, head, muzzle, ears, four legs, tail) in one dark
  colour, fur strokes masked to the body, a clipped screen gradient for the rim of light.
- **Canopy:** three dark ellipses off the top edge with `leaf` dabs scattered along their lower
  edges; ferns are `grass` stamps.

## Honest notes

- The deer is a silhouette of eleven simple shapes; the reader saw "a deer or a young fawn"
  and found the head "a very simple pointed shape". It reads, barely.
- The two trunks nearest the light are warm; the reader took them for an unfinished colour
  pass. The light should touch more things (the path's edge, the ferns) or those trunks less.
- The mist's lower edge still reads as "a hard horizontal band" to the reader.
- Trunks stop at the bottom edge of the canvas without roots or undergrowth at their feet.
- Detail 3 by the numbers: the forest's marks are its trunks and ferns, and the deer is small.
- Painting time about 2 s.

## Proposed lessons

- A misty picture gets "no light and dark side" on its mist and far layers from the critique,
  and that is right and harmless: read the finding, say why, move on.
- A drawn `rim` on a small jittered silhouette comes out as dashes; a clipped gradient on the
  lit side is the rim light for small things.
- The light must touch more than one thing: a warm colour on two trunks alone reads as a
  mistake, not as light.

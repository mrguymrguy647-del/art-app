1. A flat, stylized illustration of a misty forest in soft grey-green and cream tones. 2. My eye goes first to the small dark animal standing on the path, just right of centre. It is the darkest shape against the brightest part of the picture (the glowing fog at the end of the path), and the converging lines of the path and the trees all lead to it. The path itself is the secondary focus, pulling the eye from the bottom up toward the animal.

3. Quiet, hushed and a little melancholic, like early morning or dusk in heavy fog. The muted palette, the soft light at the end of the path and the lone animal give it a sense of stillness and solitude, with a mild tension in the way the deer seems to pause, alert, as if it has noticed something. It feels calm rather than threatening, though the dark canopy and heavy trunks add a slight sense of enclosure.

# Vermeer, Girl with a Pearl Earring

**Shapes.** A near-black background; one lit oval (the face) turned towards us; a blue
turban with a yellow fall of cloth; a yellow-brown jacket; the pearl as one tiny highlight
below the ear.

**Values** (`vermeer-girl-with-a-pearl-earring-values.png`: black 59%, dark 18%, mid 11%,
light 9%, white 3%). Four groups, with well over half the picture in the darkest band. All the
light is on the face and the collar, so the face has the strongest contrast possible: light
on black.

**Palette** (`vermeer-girl-with-a-pearl-earring-palette.png`): near-blacks and dark browns
rule; warm skin tones and the cream collar; one blue (the turban) and one grey-blue. Again
blue against yellow, in a dark key.

**Why it works.** The simplest focal design there is: one light shape on a dark field, the eye
and the pearl as the two sharpest small marks. The background is flat on purpose (a flat part
is a finding only when the part should have life). A character portrait can copy the stack
exactly: dark field, one lit face, two accents.

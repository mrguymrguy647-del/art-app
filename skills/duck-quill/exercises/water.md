# Three kinds of water

## Do

Three panels, one tree on the bank in each: a still pond (a mirror reflection, mirrored with
`"mirror": "y"` at lower opacity, fading with depth), a rippled lake (the reflection broken
into short horizontal dashes that thin out, plus light dashes on the water), and a fast stream
(dark water with white streaks that follow the current). 960 x 320. Make the rippled panel
the focal point.

The point: water is marks that follow the water's own form: horizontal where it lies flat,
along the current where it runs.

## What a good result looks like

`water.png` is one answer. `quill critique water.json` says:

- `focal point: the eye goes to rippled; inside the focal part`: the broken reflection draws
  the eye, as meant.
- `out of key: nothing` (the trees' green is in the key on purpose).
- No panel is `blotchy`: the dashes and streaks have a direction.
- Expected: the panels are `flat` inside (flat water by design); `still merges with its
  surroundings at thumbnail size` (the still pond is as pale as the sky it mirrors: true to
  life, and the reason painters darken the water a step); `the focal part touches the canvas
  edge` (the panel runs to the bottom: a layout choice, not a mistake).
